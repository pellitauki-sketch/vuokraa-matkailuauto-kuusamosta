Motorhome Hire in Kuusamo – Ruka, Lapland and Norway

Looking for a motorhome rental in northern Finland? Pellit Auki! rents a Hobby Siesta A70 GM, registered for six people. Collect it in Kuusamo or arrange delivery to Kuusamo Airport. From here, you can explore Ruka, Oulanka National Park and the wider Lapland region. Ask us about travel to Norway before booking.

The rental includes 2,000 kilometres per week. The vehicle has a kitchen, shower, toilet and heating. Weekly prices start at €790, rising to €1,280 during peak summer. See our seasonal prices.

For availability and rental details, contact us directly: +358 50 464 1906.
